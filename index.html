WordPress integration

One shortcode, with sensible defaults.

After entering your listing number under Settings → Shared House, use the basic shortcode anywhere. Attributes let you override the listing, language or season legend for an individual calendar.

  • Widget and Legacy Widget block support
  • French, English, Dutch, Spanish, German and Finnish
  • Available, booked and pending dates
  • Optional holiday-season legend
Basic shortcode
[shared_house]
Shortcode with options
[shared_house numero="132" langue="en" saisons="avec"]

Listing 132 is the plugin's demo listing. Replace it with your own Shared House listing number.
